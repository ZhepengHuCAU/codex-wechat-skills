# Ag Commodity Research Card Visual System

## Canvas and Layout

- Use 1080×1360 or 1080×1440 px for a standard portrait analytical card.
- Use outer background `#EEE9DF` and inner card `#FBFAF6` with a 30–36 px corner radius.
- Keep 34 px outer margins and 76–82 px inner horizontal padding.
- Reserve roughly 190–230 px for eyebrow, title, subtitle, and logo.
- Use a thin divider before the analytical body. Start the first panel soon after it.
- Leave 50–80 px at the bottom for sources and methodology.

## Typography

- Use Microsoft YaHei Bold for Chinese display text on Windows.
- Use Microsoft YaHei Regular for body, axes, and footnotes.
- Prefer two title lines at most. Tighten wording before reducing font size.
- Use bold selectively for conclusions, turning points, and the denominator that matters.
- Never rasterize tiny body copy merely to fit more prose. Shorten the prose.

## Logo

- Use `assets/ag-commodity-logo-transparent.png`.
- Keep its transparent background. Do not paste a white logo tile onto the off-white card.
- Set width around 205–245 px on a 1080 px card; enlarge only if it does not compete with the title.
- Align the logo to the top-right optical margin and preserve aspect ratio.

## Information Hierarchy

1. Eyebrow: English topic label in gold.
2. Headline: answer-first Chinese claim in dark green.
3. Subtitle: period, unit, and comparison basis in gray.
4. KPI or claim panel: the one number readers should remember.
5. Chart: evidence for the claim.
6. Interpretation panel: one or two concise implications.
7. Source and method: small but legible.

## Mobile Readability Test

View the full card at approximately 360 CSS px width. The headline, KPI, axis labels, key values, and interpretation must remain readable without zooming. Sources may be smaller but must still be decipherable in the original image.
